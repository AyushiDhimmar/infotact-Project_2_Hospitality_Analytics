# Hospitality Booking Analytics - Key Insights

## Booking Performance

- Total bookings exceeded 87K across City and Resort hotels.
- August recorded the highest booking volume.
- City Hotels accounted for the majority of bookings.
- Transient customers generated the highest number of reservations.

## Cancellation Analysis

- Overall cancellation rate was approximately 27%.
- Undefined market segment showed the highest cancellation rate.
- Non-Refund deposit bookings experienced the highest cancellation rate.
- Long lead-time bookings were more likely to be cancelled.
- City Hotels showed higher cancellation rates than Resort Hotels.
- Transient customers had the highest cancellation rate among customer types.
